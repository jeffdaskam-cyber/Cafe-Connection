Why it matters

Operational complexity grows when workflows are scattered.

Café and event operations across UCAR generate dozens of weekly artifacts: schedules, BEOs, sales reports, FP&A uploads, and accounting reports. Until recently, every one lived somewhere different. Cafe Connection consolidates them into a single platform that leadership, managers, and staff each see in their own way.

Before

Fragmented & manual

  • Schedules emailed as PDFs every week, by campus
  • Sales reports re-keyed into shared drives by hand
  • BEOs and event orders scattered across inboxes
  • FP&A reporting required reassembling the same data multiple times
  • No single view senior leaders could trust on demand
After

Connected & live

  • Schedules pull live from Google Sheets, by campus and role
  • Sales reports parse on upload — Firestore updated in seconds
  • BEOs, setups, and weekly packets in one searchable library
  • FP&A dashboards always current — 8 visuals, FYTD + rolling
  • Leadership signs in once and sees the full picture

Capabilities

Everything operational, in one place.

The platform combines front-line operational support with structured reporting and leadership visibility. Each capability reinforces the others.

Operational Workspace
Schedules, exceptions, cash drops, specials, and event coordination — all in a campus-selectable weekly view.
Event Coordination
BEOs, setup reports, event orders, and weekly packet exports bring every event document into one searchable library.
Sales Reporting
Cafe sales, event revenue, and monthly accounting outputs — exportable as Excel reports with one click.
Executive Dashboards
FP&A visibility, rolling financial trends, and eight Workday-based visuals — FYTD and 13-month rolling views.
Role-Based Access
Four access tiers — User, Manager, Senior Leader, Administrator — ensure visibility is proportional to responsibility.

Mobile experience

Built for the floor, not just the office.

The mobile layout of Cafe Connection puts the four most-needed views a single tap away.

  • Staff Schedule

    Current week's schedule by campus. Who's in, who's remote, and WFH flags, in one glance.

  • Cafe Specials

    Weekly specials pulled live from Google Drive.

  • Ops Quick-Actions

    Log cash drops and access operational tools directly from the floor, without navigating the full desktop app.

  • Personal Dashboard

    A condensed view of today's sales, team activity, and key metrics, all optimized for mobile devices.

Mobile layout activates automatically. No separate app to install or maintain.
Architecture & trust

Built like a real internal product.

Modern cloud foundation, secure UCAR-only authentication, role-based access, and clean integrations with the systems UCAR already runs on.

How it fits together

End-to-end flow from a UCAR sign-in to dashboards leadership can act on.

Controlled access

UCAR-only, role-aware

Magic-link sign-in restricted to @ucar.edu, plus four permission tiers. Visibility matches user needs.

Cloud-native

Built to scale, day one

React on Vercel, Firestore for live data, Firebase Auth for identity — a modern stack with a proven track record in production environments.

Integrates, doesn't replace

Lives in your existing stack

Reads schedules from Google Sheets, retrieves documents from Drive, and ingests Workday exports — strengthening the tools UCAR already runs on.
