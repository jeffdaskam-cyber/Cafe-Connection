Why it matters

Operational complexity grows quickly when workflows are scattered

Café and event operations generate a steady stream of schedules, event documents, specials, financial files, uploads, and reporting needs. When those workflows are fragmented across tools and handoffs, visibility slows down and operational effort increases. Cafe Connection brings those workflows together in a more efficient digital environment.

Before connection

Operational information lives in separate documents, file locations, and manual processes. Reporting takes more coordination, context is harder to preserve, and leadership visibility depends on extra effort.

After connection

A connected internal platform creates clearer access to workflows, cleaner reporting pathways, and a stronger digital foundation for how café and event services operate across campuses.

What it is

A centralized internal platform for operational execution and executive visibility

Cafe Connection is UCAR’s internal platform for supporting café and event operations across campuses. It centralizes operational workflows, reporting tools, executive dashboards, and role-based access in one connected system.

Built for real operations

The platform supports the day-to-day needs of café and event teams with practical workflow tools, document access, reporting support, and centralized information flow.

Designed to scale in value

Cafe Connection is not positioned as a static utility. It is a starting point for broader integration, smarter reporting, and a more connected operating model over time.
